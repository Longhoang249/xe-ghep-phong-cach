## GEO questions for Long

1. Which Facebook Page, Zalo OA/profile, Google Business Profile, TikTok and YouTube properties are officially owned by Phong?
2. Are name, phone, service area, payment policy and booking terms identical across every official surface?
3. Which local entities can provide verifiable citations or reviews without inventing coverage, prices or schedules?
4. Is there an operational process for publishing endpoint availability changes without creating claims that conflict with the Route Knowledge Base?
5. Can genuine customer questions/reviews be categorized by route and endpoint, then handed back as research evidence without exposing personal data?

## Do not carry into GEO as facts

- Competitor prices, hours, frequency, pickup zones or endpoint coverage.
- Mirrored social posts without verification.
- Any claim that Phong serves Hạ Long, Bãi Cháy, Đông Triều, Cẩm Phả, Vân Đồn, Móng Cái, Uông Bí, Quảng Yên or Ao Tiên until Owner confirmation.
