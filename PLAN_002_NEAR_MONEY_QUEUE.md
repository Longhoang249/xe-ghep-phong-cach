## Executive decision

The next commercial wave should not be another batch of supporting articles. It should open permission to upgrade the two existing money pages:

1. MP-003 - `/xe-ghep-hai-duong-hai-phong`
2. MP-005 - `/xe-ghep-hai-duong-quang-ninh`

Those pages should own corridor-level **price, xe ghép, bao xe, door-to-door, both-direction and booking** intent. Their approved upgrade packages already contain the verified business facts and the necessary separation between a starting price and a fixed price.

SC-001 is the only additional existing asset recommended for the near-money queue. It may continue to own the distinct booking-process question for Hải Dương - Quảng Ninh, but only after MP-005 has become the canonical commercial answer. It must not become another price or service landing page.

No new exact-match price, charter, door-to-door, group-size or reverse-direction URL is recommended. Search snapshots repeatedly collapsed these variants into operator landing pages, price sections, booking forms and comparison/list pages. Creating separate URLs would divide evidence, links and conversion signals across pages that solve the same underlying task.

**Strategic implication:** continuing to avoid MP-003/MP-005 edits now slows the move from traffic to commercial search. If money-page editing remains closed, the safe response is to pause near-money production rather than route the same intent through more blog URLs.

## Research discipline

- Live search snapshots were checked on 2026-08-22 in Vietnamese-market results.
- Results are directional snapshots, not a permanent ranking report. They can vary by location, device, personalization and time.
- Search volume is `UNKNOWN`. No number is inferred from result density or competitor recurrence.
- Competitor and aggregator amounts are used only to identify SERP presentation patterns. They are not Phong Cách prices or operational evidence.
- Aggregator operator counts, trip counts and similar totals are not copied because consistency was not established.
- Only Owner-provenanced `VERIFIED` and `VERIFIED_FROM` facts are treated as commercial truth for Phong Cách.

### SERP synthesis

1. Commercial winners combine direct answers, prices or quote prompts, service scope and a visible contact/booking action.
2. Price is a section of the service decision, not a reliably separate editorial intent in these two corridors.
3. Group-size wording describes a decision the money page should solve. It does not justify a standalone article from the current snapshot.
4. Reverse queries still express the same bidirectional service. A separate direction page would duplicate the offer and evidence.
5. For Quảng Ninh, the opportunity is not “more endpoint prices.” It is a clearer distinction between parent-corridor starting price, exact address and unconfirmed endpoint availability.

## Queue A - DO NEXT

Three assets are enough. The queue is intentionally not padded to 4-6 URLs.

### 1. MP-003 - upgrade existing money page

- Own: price, xe ghép, bao xe, group choice, door-to-door, reverse and booking CTA.
- Use: the approved `VERIFIED_FROM` prices and verified service/policy facts above.
- Add: answer-first price block, 4/7-seat choice, short “ghép hay bao xe” table, exact booking inputs, natural call/Zalo path and CP-003 support link.
- Keep unknown: hours, frequency, lead time, waiting and surcharges.
- No new URL.

### 2. MP-005 - upgrade existing parent money page

- Own: parent-corridor price, xe ghép, bao xe, parent door-to-door, reverse and booking CTA.
- Use: only parent `VERIFIED_FROM` amounts and verified parent service/policy facts.
- Add: prominent explanation that the starting price is not one endpoint fare; ask for the exact Quảng Ninh destination/address.
- Endpoint names may help orientation, but do not assert availability or publish endpoint prices.
- No new URL.

### 3. SC-001 - upgrade existing booking-process guide after MP-005

- Own: how to request a trip and what details to provide.
- Safe inputs: date, desired time, exact pickup, exact destination, passenger count, luggage or parcel details, and preferred shared/private service.
- Link the commercial decision and price answer to MP-005.
- Do not publish lead-time, fixed availability, schedule, waiting or endpoint promises.
- No new URL.
