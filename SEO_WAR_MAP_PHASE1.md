# RES-002 — SEO Battlefield / War Map Phase 1

Status: `REVIEW`

Research date: 2026-08-21 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh)

Public website/content changes: none

## Executive decision

The first market move should strengthen the four existing Phase 1 route assets and their comparison/support layer before creating many endpoint URLs. The best near-term gap is **Hải Dương ⇄ Quảng Ninh parent-route intent**: commercial and price demand is repeatedly served by exact pages, but several visible results are broad, stale or unclear about endpoint/fare scope.

**Hải Phòng ⇄ Hạ Long** shows the clearest independent endpoint battle, but it is not a quick win: aggregators, booking operators and specialist pages already serve it well. **Hải Dương ⇄ Hạ Long** is the strongest lower-competition endpoint candidate. Neither may enter production until Owner confirms actual service, directions, modes, areas and fare/quote rules.

No evidence supports a page factory for every Quảng Ninh endpoint. Reverse directions should normally be served by one strong bidirectional asset.

## Evidence and scoring discipline

Live search snapshots were checked on 2026-08-21. They are directional evidence, not a permanent rank record. Results can vary by location, device, personalization and time.

No GSC, Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or equivalent volume source was available, so no search-volume number is estimated. Opportunity ordering uses a transparent internal model:

`20 × (25% Search Evidence + 25% Commercial Intent + 20% SERP Weakness + 20% Business Fit + 10% Data Readiness)`

Every input is 0–5. Unconfirmed endpoints are capped at Business Fit 3/5. The score is not a Google metric or traffic forecast.

## Battlefield by cluster

### Cluster A — Hải Dương ⇄ Hải Phòng and Cát Bi

**SERP shape:** exact operators, a fresh comparison article and bus aggregators compete on the core corridor. Price tables and FAQs are common. A dedicated reverse page ranks for Hải Phòng → Hải Dương, but the commercial service remains substantively bidirectional.

**Opening:** Phong already has verified parent-route shared/private values and verified door-to-door, two-way, payment-after-trip and free-advance-booking claims. MP-003 can give clearer fare scope and operational truth than generic/stale competitor claims without creating a reverse duplicate.

**Cát Bi:** exact airport pricing and reverse-transfer pages prove independent commercial intent. MP-004 and CP-007 should be upgraded only after waiting, flight-delay, terminal pickup, surcharge and lead-time rules are verified. [Vietnam Airlines’ Cát Bi guide](https://www.vietnamairlines.com/go/vi/plan-book/travel/travel-guide/san-bay-cat-bi) is an authoritative informational source for future airport facts, not a competitor service fact.

**Decision:** upgrade existing assets; do not split reverse direction. Consider later consolidation of SC-004 into MP-003 because its reverse framing overlaps the money page.

### Cluster B — Hải Dương ⇄ Quảng Ninh

**SERP shape:** exact corridor pages recur, led by [ghephaiduong.com](https://www.ghephaiduong.com/), an older [BusVietnam list](https://busvietnam.net/xe-ghep/xe-ghep-hai-duong-quang-ninh.html), a broad [operator corridor page](https://xeghephaiduongdicactinh24h.com/hai-duong-quang-ninh/), and reverse/endpoint aggregators. Several operator surfaces are stale or broad; exact endpoint coverage is uneven.

**Opening:** MP-005 is the clearest quick win. It should own broad commercial, price, reverse and door-to-door intent while honestly distinguishing verified parent facts from unconfirmed endpoint facts.

**Endpoint ladder:**

1. **Hạ Long:** strongest new candidate; exact commercial competitor and repeated route evidence.
2. **Đông Triều / Bãi Cháy / Cẩm Phả / Vân Đồn:** real market evidence exists, but it is less consistent and remains backlog until Owner truth is captured.
3. **Móng Cái:** independent long-distance evidence exists, but operational fit is especially uncertain.
4. **Uông Bí:** broad results but insufficient independent evidence for a standalone URL.
5. **Quảng Yên / Ao Tiên:** no standalone investment from current evidence.

Hạ Long and Bãi Cháy must start as one cannibalization decision. Ao Tiên belongs with Vân Đồn unless future evidence proves otherwise.

### Cluster C — Hải Phòng ⇄ Quảng Ninh

**SERP shape:** broad corridor results mix xe ghép operators, coaches, limousine comparison articles and aggregators. MP-006 faces stronger competition than MP-005.

**Opening:** strengthen MP-006’s verified parent service proposition; use CP-004 for mode/operator comparison rather than overloading the money page.

**Hạ Long battle:** [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/vi-vn/bus-and-shuttle/route/ve-xe-hai-phong-city.ha-long-city), [redBus](https://www.redbus.vn/ve-xe-khach/tuyen-duong/hai-phong-di-ha-long), [Hoàng Phú](https://xehoangphu.vn/dat-ve/dat-xe-hai-phong-ha-long/), [Dailyve](https://dailyve.com.vn/ve-xe-khach/tuyen-duong/hai-phong-di-ha-long/) and specialist operators create a distinct, strong SERP. This supports a future endpoint URL only if Phong truly serves it with a differentiated door-to-door/shared/private proposition.

## Recurring SERP patterns

1. **Prices are expected on commercial pages.** Tables recur, but provenance and service scope are often unclear. Verified values plus explicit “Liên hệ” are safer differentiation than copying endpoint prices.
2. **Aggregators win inventory intent.** Vexere, redBus and Traveloka offer schedule/fare comparison; Phong should win on first-party operational clarity rather than pretending to be a marketplace.
3. **Comparison publishers win “xe nào/nhà xe nào”.** Existing CP assets are the correct containers for mode/operator evaluation.
4. **Hạ Long is a separate search battlefield.** It has multiple dedicated pages and query variants in both relevant corridors.
5. **Other endpoints are uneven.** One aggregator page or one parent mention is evidence for an owner question, not automatic permission to create a URL.
6. **Reverse intent usually overlaps.** The sampled result sets do not justify separate directional Phong pages.
7. **Social discovery exists but is fragmented.** Link hubs, marketplace profiles, fanpage links and mirrored posts surfaced; details are handed to GEO rather than implemented here.

## Opportunity buckets

### Quick wins

- **MP-005:** Hải Dương ⇄ Quảng Ninh parent commercial and price intent.
- **MP-003:** Hải Dương ⇄ Hải Phòng verified price/charter/reverse/door-to-door coverage.
- **CP-003:** mode-choice support that reinforces MP-003 without duplicating booking intent.

### Strategic battles

- **MP-006 + CP-004:** broad Hải Phòng ⇄ Quảng Ninh.
- **MP-004 + CP-007:** Cát Bi, gated by airport operating rules.
- **Conditional new Hải Phòng ⇄ Hạ Long page:** strong demand evidence and strong competition.

### Long-tail saturation

- Conditional Hải Dương ⇄ Hạ Long first.
- Đông Triều, Bãi Cháy, Cẩm Phả, Vân Đồn and Móng Cái remain evidence-backed backlog, not approved pages.

### Do not pursue

- Standalone Quảng Yên or Ao Tiên.
- Standalone Uông Bí from current evidence.
- Separate reverse-direction URLs.
- Parcel expansion before business verification.
- Programmatic endpoint/district pages created only because generation is easy.
